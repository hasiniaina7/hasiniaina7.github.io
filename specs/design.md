# Design specification — Agentic editorial system

## Direction

Preserve the established refined technical-editorial identity: white and soft-blue surfaces, navy text, blue-violet atmospheric accents, Deep Teal actions, real portrait, system diagrams, and restrained premium motion.

The memorable interaction is the recruiter path from orchestration promise → visible seven-stage delivery rail → three proof cases → resume/contact action.

## Hierarchy

- The home hero prioritizes positioning, promise, level, location, remote availability, relocation openness, and recruiter CTAs.
- The primary CTA opens the Agentic Delivery page.
- The primary download is the AI/RAG/Automation resume in the active locale.
- The Full-Stack resume is visibly secondary.
- Existing product work follows the agentic proof block, not the reverse.

## Shared shell

- Keep the portrait brand mark and sticky header.
- Add a compact EN/FR switch that preserves the equivalent route.
- Keep navigation at six items per locale.
- Use localized skip link, navigation labels, contact action, footer labels, and loading state.

## Agentic Delivery composition

- Render seven connected stages on wide screens and an ordered semantic list on mobile.
- Show Claude Code as coordinator and Codex as secondary support.
- Render human approval gates as visually distinct, high-contrast cards.
- Render TZ Smart, Fretunia, and ACM/iagasy as equal qualitative case summaries.
- Do not allocate space for a video when its required assets are absent.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- No horizontal overflow from 360 to 1440 px.
- One H1 per route.
- Visible focus, keyboard-operable menu/switch/filter/lightbox, 44 px minimum action targets.
- Semantic ordered stages, descriptive labels, and live announcements where state changes.
- Preserve content and remove non-essential movement under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep contrast at WCAG AA or better.
